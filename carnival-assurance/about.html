About Carnival Assurance

We believe in 'Happiness Together' — Building trust through honest and transparent insurance solutions since 2020.

Our Story

From Trust to Triumph

Carnival Assurance started from 06th June 2020. Before starting officially, we already had strong customer trust and experience in the insurance field. Today we proudly serve 800+ clients across various cities of India.

We believe in 'HAPPINESS TOGETHER'. We are known for fast quotation service, quick claim support, transparent communication, honest service, and good faith relationships with clients.

Customer satisfaction and trust are our biggest strengths. Our founder, Kirankumar Deshbattin, envisioned an insurance advisory that puts people first — and that vision drives everything we do.
