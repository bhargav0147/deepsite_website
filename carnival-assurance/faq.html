Claims & Process

Claims & Policy Questions

How does the claim process work?
+
Our claim process is simple. Contact us via WhatsApp or phone, share the details of your claim, and we will guide you through the entire process step by step. We provide 24-hour claim assistance to ensure you get your benefits quickly and without hassle.
How long does claim settlement take?
+
Claim settlement times vary by insurance type and insurer. However, we actively follow up with insurance companies to expedite the process. Health insurance claims with cashless hospitalization are typically settled within a few hours, while life insurance claims may take 15-30 days.
Can I switch my existing insurance to Carnival Assurance?
+
Absolutely! If you have an existing policy and want better service or a more suitable plan, we can help you transition smoothly. We'll review your current policy, compare options, and recommend the best path forward — all at no cost to you.
What documents do I need to buy insurance?
+
The documents needed vary by insurance type. Generally, you'll need identity proof (Aadhaar/PAN), address proof, and income proof for life insurance. For motor insurance, you need your vehicle's RC book. We'll guide you through the exact requirements during our consultation.
What makes Carnival Assurance different?
+
We stand out for our fast quotation service, quick claim support, transparent communication, honest service, and good faith relationships with clients. Customer satisfaction and trust are our biggest strengths. We never upsell or recommend what isn't best for you.
