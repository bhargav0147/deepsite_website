Welcome to Hotel Akbari

A Comfortable Stay in the Heart of Cuttack

Hotel Akbari is located at Dolmundai, Haripur Road, Mangalabag, Cuttack, Odisha — one of the most accessible and well-connected areas in the city. Whether you're visiting for business, leisure, or a family trip, our hotel offers the perfect base for your stay.

We pride ourselves on providing a comfortable and convenient experience for every guest. Our air-conditioned rooms are clean, well-maintained, and equipped with essential amenities including free Wi-Fi and daily housekeeping. Start your mornings with our complimentary breakfast and rest easy knowing our 24-hour front desk is always available to assist you.

At Hotel Akbari, we believe hospitality is about more than just a room — it's about creating a welcoming environment where guests feel valued and cared for. Our dedicated team is committed to ensuring your stay is pleasant, hassle-free, and memorable.
