Amenities & Services

Facilities Designed for Your Comfort

Everything you need for a relaxing and hassle-free stay at Hotel Akbari, Cuttack.

Free Wi-Fi

Stay connected with complimentary high-speed internet access available in all rooms and public areas throughout the hotel.

Car Park

Free and secure parking space available for all hotel guests. Park your vehicle with peace of mind during your stay.

24-Hour Front Desk

Our friendly staff is available around the clock to assist you with check-in, inquiries, and any needs during your stay.

Luggage Storage

Store your bags safely before check-in or after check-out. Our secure luggage storage lets you explore the city freely.

Free Breakfast

Start your day right with our complimentary breakfast served every morning. Enjoy a variety of fresh and local dishes.

Laundry

Professional laundry and dry-cleaning services available so you can travel light and always have fresh clothes ready.

Rental Car

Need a vehicle to explore Cuttack? We can help arrange rental cars at competitive rates for your convenience.

Daily Housekeeping

Enjoy a clean and tidy room every day with our thorough daily housekeeping service. Fresh linens and towels provided.

Air Conditioning

All rooms come equipped with air conditioning to keep you cool and comfortable, especially during Odisha's warm summers.
