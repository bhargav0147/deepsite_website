Sanjay Agarwal founded Yashasvee Spiritual Private Limited in 2008 with a singular mission: to help people create balanced, prosperous environments through scientific Vaastu solutions without the need for destructive renovations.

With over 18 years of dedicated practice and research, he has developed a unique methodology that combines the ancient science of Vaastu Shastra with modern energy-balancing techniques. His approach focuses on the strategic placement of Pyramid Yantras and other energized remedies to correct Vaastu defects.

Having consulted over 5000 clients across Gujarat and beyond, Sanjay Agarwal has established himself as one of the most trusted names in the field of Vaastu consultancy. His 90%+ success ratio speaks volumes about the effectiveness of his scientific approach.
