[ ANGEL CAPITAL & VENTURE ADVISORY ]

Angel Investment Thesis

Supporting high-conviction founders building scalable, tech-enabled solutions with strategic positioning and reputation advisory.

Capital Enhanced by Strategic Positioning

As an active angel investor, Harsh Savaliya approaches early-stage venture backing through a dual lens of business fundamentals and public narrative potential.

Great technology and ambitious product roadmaps require clear positioning to win market share, attract exceptional talent, and secure follow-on capital. Harsh collaborates with portfolio founders to ensure their company narrative matches their engineering execution.

Core Investment Focus

  • ⚡ Early-Stage Startups: Seed and pre-series A technology-driven enterprises.
  • 🌐 Digital Identity & SaaS: Platforms enabling workflow automation, digital infrastructure, and consumer tech.
  • 🧠 High-Conviction Founders: Relentless operators solving real, scalable market inefficiencies.
[ EVALUATION CRITERIA ]

What I Look for in Founders

Harsh Savaliya evaluates investment opportunities based on fundamental business health, founder resilience, and market expansion capacity.

CRITERIA 01

Founder Resilience & Grit

Looking for founders with deep domain understanding, adaptability under pressure, and absolute clarity on the problem they are solving.

CRITERIA 02

Large Addressable Market

Vventures targeting expanding sectors with structural tailwinds, high customer lifetime value, and international scalability potential.

CRITERIA 03

Product & Tech Moats

Defensible technology, network effects, proprietary data, or unique workflow integrations that create high switching costs for users.

CRITERIA 04

Sustainable Unit Economics

Focusing on business models built around sound unit economics, disciplined capital deployment, and path to profitability over vanity growth.

CRITERIA 05

Strategic Value Alignment

Seeking teams where Harsh's background in PR strategy, digital credibility, and executive branding can directly accelerate growth.

CRITERIA 06

Ethical Foundation

High integrity, transparent governance, and a commitment to building a durable enterprise with sustainable stakeholder trust.
