Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about AtomGold

What is digital gold and how does it work?

Digital gold is a method of investing in 24K physical gold that is stored in secure vaults on your behalf. When you buy digital gold on AtomGold, an equivalent amount of physical gold is purchased and stored in RBI-approved vaults. You own this gold and can sell it anytime or request physical delivery.

How does the 100% free gold offer work?

When you buy digital gold, AtomGold adds bonus gold to your account based on your purchase amount. Buy ₹1,000 to get 5% extra, ₹10,000 for 25% extra, and ₹1,00,000 for a whopping 100% extra gold. This bonus gold is credited instantly to your account.

Is my gold safe in AtomGold vaults?

Absolutely! Your gold is stored in RBI-approved, fully insured vaults with 24/7 security monitoring. All transactions are protected with 256-bit encryption and two-factor authentication. The physical gold is audited regularly to ensure complete safety.

Can I sell my gold anytime?

Yes, you can sell your digital gold anytime at prevailing market rates. The amount is credited directly to your bank account within 24 hours. There are no lock-in periods or withdrawal restrictions.

What is the minimum amount to invest?

You can start investing in digital gold with as little as ₹1. There is no maximum limit, so you can invest any amount you're comfortable with. This makes it easy for everyone to start their gold investment journey.

Are there any hidden fees or charges?

No, AtomGold operates with complete transparency. The price you see is the price you pay. There are no hidden fees, no storage charges, and no management fees. The only cost is the minimal spread between buy and sell prices, which is among the lowest in the industry.
