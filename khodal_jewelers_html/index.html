Our Story

Three Decades of Craftsmanship & Trust

Established in 1995, Khodal Jewelers has been Surat's trusted destination for premium gold and silver jewelry. Our legacy is built on unwavering commitment to purity, exceptional craftsmanship, and customer satisfaction.

We specialize in creating exquisite necklaces, bangles, earrings, and complete bridal collections. With our direct-to-customer approach, we offer retail pricing without compromising on quality—making luxury jewelry accessible to every family.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

We use 22 karat and 18 karat gold for our jewelry, which is the standard for premium Indian jewelry. All our gold pieces are BIS hallmarked, ensuring authenticity and purity. We also offer 24 karat gold coins for investment purposes.

Custom jewelry typically takes 2-4 weeks depending on the complexity of the design. For wedding collections or elaborate pieces, we recommend placing orders 6-8 weeks in advance to ensure timely delivery with no compromise on quality.

Yes, absolutely! All our gold and silver jewelry comes with BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) hallmark certification. This guarantees the purity of metal and is your assurance of quality and authenticity.

Our pricing is completely transparent. We charge based on daily gold rates plus making charges, which vary based on design complexity. We offer competitive retail pricing without middlemen, ensuring you get the best value for your investment.
