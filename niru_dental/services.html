What We Offer

Complete Dental Solutions

From routine check-ups to advanced cosmetic procedures, we provide a full spectrum of dental services.

Preventive Dentistry

Teeth Cleaning

Professional scaling and polishing to remove plaque, tartar, and stains for a fresh, clean smile.

  • Ultrasonic scaling
  • Polishing & fluoride
  • Gum health assessment

Dental Check-up

Comprehensive oral examination to detect problems early and maintain optimal oral health.

  • Complete oral exam
  • Digital X-rays
  • Treatment planning

Fluoride Treatment

Preventive fluoride application to strengthen enamel and protect against tooth decay.

  • Enamel strengthening
  • Cavity prevention
  • Kids & adults

Restorative Dentistry

Root Canal Treatment

Painless root canal therapy to save infected teeth and eliminate pain using modern techniques.

  • Painless procedure
  • Save natural tooth
  • Permanent restoration

Dental Implants

Permanent tooth replacement with titanium implants that look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

  • Permanent solution
  • Natural appearance
  • Long-lasting results

Dental Fillings

Tooth-colored composite fillings that restore damaged teeth while maintaining a natural appearance.

  • Tooth-colored match
  • Durable & strong
  • Mercury-free options

Crowns & Bridges

Custom-made dental crowns and bridges to restore damaged or missing teeth to full function.

  • Custom-fitted
  • Natural-looking
  • Long-lasting

Tooth Extraction

Gentle and painless tooth removal when necessary, with minimal discomfort and quick recovery.

  • Painless extraction
  • Wisdom teeth
  • Quick recovery

Dentures

Custom full or partial dentures to restore your smile and improve eating and speaking ability.

  • Full & partial options
  • Comfortable fit
  • Natural appearance

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth Whitening

Professional teeth whitening to brighten your smile by several shades safely and effectively.

  • In-office whitening
  • Take-home kits
  • Safe & effective

Veneers

Thin porcelain shells bonded to the front of teeth to correct chips, gaps, and discoloration.

  • Hollywood smile
  • Stain-resistant
  • Minimally invasive

Braces & Aligners

Orthodontic solutions including traditional braces and clear aligners for perfectly aligned teeth.

  • Metal & ceramic braces
  • Clear aligners
  • All ages welcome
