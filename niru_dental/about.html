Our Story

A Journey of Smiles Since 2021

Niru Dental Clinic was founded in 2021 by Dr. Niru Patel with a simple yet powerful mission — to provide high-quality dental care that is accessible, comfortable, and stress-free for every patient.

Located on Main Road, Songadh, Gujarat, our clinic has grown from a small practice into a trusted dental care center serving thousands of happy patients. We believe that everyone deserves a healthy, beautiful smile, and we work tirelessly to make that happen.

Our approach combines modern dental technology with genuine compassion. We take the time to listen to your concerns, explain treatment options clearly, and ensure you feel comfortable throughout your visit. At Niru Dental Clinic, you're not just a patient — you're family.

Our Mission

To provide comprehensive, high-quality dental care in a warm and welcoming environment. We are committed to making dental treatments painless, affordable, and accessible to every patient, ensuring that fear or cost never comes between you and a healthy smile.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and preferred dental clinic in Songadh and surrounding areas, known for clinical excellence, patient satisfaction, and innovative dental solutions. We envision a community where everyone smiles with confidence and optimal oral health.

Meet the Founder

Dr. Niru Patel

Founder & Lead Dentist

Dr. Niru Patel is a passionate dental professional dedicated to transforming smiles and improving oral health. With extensive training in preventive, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Patel brings a unique combination of clinical skill and heartfelt compassion to every patient interaction.

After completing her BDS degree, Dr. Patel founded Niru Dental Clinic in 2021 with the goal of creating a dental practice where patients feel genuinely cared for. Her gentle approach, especially with anxious patients, has earned her a reputation as one of the most trusted dentists in the Songadh region.
