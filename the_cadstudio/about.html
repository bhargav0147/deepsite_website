About The Cadstudio

A forward-thinking design and engineering studio dedicated to transforming ideas into impactful realities.

Our Story

Building the Future Since 2017

The Cadstudio was founded in 2019 by Vivek Wagh with a singular vision: to bridge the gap between creative ideas and engineered realities through cutting-edge CAD technology and consultancy.

What began as a one-person consultancy has grown into a trusted name in CAD design, product development, architectural planning, and interior design solutions. Our philosophy centers on three core pillars: innovation, precision, and responsibility.

Every project we undertake reflects our commitment to pushing the boundaries of what's possible while maintaining the highest standards of accuracy and client satisfaction. We believe that great design isn't just about aesthetics — it's about creating solutions that work flawlessly in the real world.

Leadership

Meet the Founder

Vivek Wagh

Founder & Lead Consultant

With over 10 years of experience in CAD design and engineering consultancy, Vivek founded The Cadstudio with a mission to deliver premium design solutions that combine technical precision with creative innovation. His expertise spans across product development, architectural planning, and industrial design, making him a versatile consultant for projects of all scales.

Vivek's approach emphasizes collaboration, attention to detail, and a relentless pursuit of excellence — values that have become the cornerstone of The Cadstudio's identity.
