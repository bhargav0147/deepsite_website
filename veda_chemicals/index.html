About Veda Enterprises

Our Mission

To provide innovative chemical solutions that enhance industrial processes while maintaining the highest standards of safety and environmental responsibility.

Our Vision

To be the preferred partner for specialty chemicals globally, recognized for our technical expertise, reliability, and commitment to sustainability.

Our Values

Integrity, Innovation, Excellence, Sustainability, and Customer Focus drive everything we do at Veda Enterprises.

Our Story

Founded in 2005, Veda Enterprises began as a small chemical distributor with a vision to transform the industry. Today, we're a leading manufacturer with state-of-the-art facilities and a team of expert chemists.

Our journey has been marked by continuous innovation, strategic partnerships, and an unwavering commitment to quality. We take pride in our ability to develop customized solutions for our clients' most challenging requirements.

Key Milestones

  • 2005: Company founded with 5 employees
  • 2012: First manufacturing facility established
  • 2018: ISO 9001 and 14001 certifications achieved
  • 2023: Expanded to international markets
