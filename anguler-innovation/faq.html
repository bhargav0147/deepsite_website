Clear, transparent answers regarding our Public Relations procedures, Google Knowledge Panel strategy, custom software engineering standards, retainers, and security.

What makes Anguler Innovation different from traditional PR agencies?
+
Unlike conventional PR agencies that rely solely on sending generic press releases, we combine strategic media placements with digital entity reconciliation and full-stack software development. We build the digital authority assets and high-performance websites that convert your media exposure into tangible business growth.
How do you approach Google Knowledge Panel recognition?
+
We follow Google's structured entity guidelines. We audit your brand presence, implement verified JSON-LD schema markup, harmonize citations across Wikidata and accredited press, and build organic eligibility. We never make false promises of permanent placement or guaranteed instant verification.
Why do you build with pure HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript?
+
Pure web standards eliminate dependency bloat, security vulnerabilities, and heavy framework overhead. Vanilla code loads instantly, achieves 95+ Core Web Vitals scores on Google Lighthouse, ensures seamless mobile responsiveness, and remains easy to maintain for years without breaking build tools.
Can you build full-stack custom SaaS and mobile applications?
+
Yes. Our technology division designs and engineers production-ready Android/iOS mobile apps, subscription SaaS platforms with Stripe billing, multi-tenant databases, custom enterprise CRMs, and automated API backends.
What are your typical engagement models and retainer terms?
+
For Public Relations and Digital Authority, we operate on 3 to 6-month strategic retainers to build sustainable entity authority. For Web and Software engineering, we offer fixed-scope milestone delivery or dedicated monthly sprint retainers depending on project scale.
How do I start a project with Anguler Innovation?

Simply submit your project details via our Contact Form or email us at admin@anguler.com. Our directors will review your requirements and schedule a strategic consultation call within 24 hours.
