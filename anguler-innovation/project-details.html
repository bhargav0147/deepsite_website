The Challenge

Nexus Cloud required a complete technology overhaul to support enterprise B2B customers while establishing instant executive trust in a crowded cloud analytics market. Their previous legacy website lacked mobile responsiveness, loaded slowly, and had zero media press authority.

The Dual Strategy

Anguler Innovation deployed a simultaneous dual-track engagement strategy:

  • 1. Technology Track: Engineered a lightweight, ultra-fast web interface using pure HTML5, CSS grid/flexbox, and Vanilla JS, connected to a microservices cloud backend.
  • 2. PR Track: Formulated an executive founder personal branding campaign around cloud data privacy, pitching thought leadership op-eds to tier-one tech journals.

The Results & Impact

Within 90 days of launch, Nexus secured 18 accredited editorial media placements, achieved Google Knowledge Graph entity recognition for its executive team, and converted over 120 enterprise trials into paid subscriptions.
