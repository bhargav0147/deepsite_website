Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Inquiries

Everything you need to know about working with Anguler Innovation.

How does Anguler Innovation combine PR with technology development?
+
We operate as a single unified agency. While our technology engineering team builds your web platforms, SaaS products, or mobile apps, our PR strategists build your executive identity, press exposure, and search entity authority. This creates immediate trust when potential clients or investors look up your new product.
Do you guarantee Google Knowledge Panels or specific press rankings?
+
We strictly adhere to ethical PR practices and Google guidelines. We do not make false promises of permanent placement or guaranteed verification. Instead, we structure verifiable digital entity data, press placements across recognized publications, and schema markup that aligns with Google Knowledge Graph eligibility requirements.
What technologies do you use for website and software development?
+
We specialize in clean, fast, vanilla web standards (HTML5, modern CSS3, modular JavaScript) for maximum performance, zero bloat, and long-term security. For complex software and mobile backends, we build robust microservice APIs, cloud-native serverless setups, and database solutions tailored to your operational scale.
What is your typical project timeline?
+
Strategic PR and personal branding roadmaps usually run over 3 to 6-month retainers to establish genuine entity authority. Corporate website development takes approximately 3 to 6 weeks, while complex SaaS or mobile app engineering ranges from 8 to 16 weeks depending on feature scope.
