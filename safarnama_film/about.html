About Us

The story behind the lens — passion, artistry, and an eye for the details that matter most.

Meet The Founder

Ravi Kanani

With over 15 years of dedicated experience in wedding photography and cinematic filmmaking, Ravi Kanani founded Safarnama Film with a singular vision — to capture not just how a wedding looks, but how it feels.

His approach blends artistic storytelling with technical precision, ensuring every frame carries the weight of genuine emotion. From the quiet glance between a bride and her father to the exuberant celebration on the dance floor, Ravi's lens finds the moments that matter.

"We don't just capture weddings.
We capture feelings you'll relive forever."

Based in Surat, Gujarat, Ravi and his talented team travel across India and internationally to document love stories in their most authentic form. Every wedding is a new canvas, and Safarnama Film paints it with light, emotion, and cinematic beauty.
