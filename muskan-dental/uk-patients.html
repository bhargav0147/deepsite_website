Your Timeline

The 7-Day Smile Journey

Day 1Arrival

Welcome to Dubai

Airport pickup, hotel check-in, evening consultation with your treatment team. Final treatment planning and 3D imaging are completed.

Day 2Surgery

Implant Procedure

Implant placement under sedation followed by a short recovery. Temporary teeth are fitted, allowing you to smile confidently during your journey.

Day 3–7Umrah

Travel for Umrah

Travel to Makkah and Madinah for your 4–5 day Umrah pilgrimage. With your temporary teeth in place, you can comfortably continue your spiritual journey while your implants begin healing.

Day 8Return

Return to Dubai & Recovery Check

Return to Dubai for a follow-up examination. Your implants and healing progress are evaluated before proceeding with the final smile adjustments.

Day 9Fitting

Final Restoration

Your bite is fine-tuned, aesthetics are perfected, and your final smile is carefully adjusted for comfort and function.

Day 10Departure

New Smile, New Journey

Final check-up, personalized care instructions, virtual follow-up scheduling, and complimentary airport transfer for your journey home.
