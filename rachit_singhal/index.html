The Journey

A Story of Passion & Precision

Rachit Singhal is an award-winning visual storyteller, photographer, cinematographer, and licensed drone operator based in New Delhi, India with a strong global outlook. With over seven years of experience he has worked across sports, large-scale events, architecture, lifestyle, hospitality, fashion, and broadcast media.

His portfolio includes collaborations with brands and organizations such as Red Bull, Marriott Bonvoy, Walmart India, Taco Bell India, Embassy of Switzerland, Prohelvetia, CGTN News, and World Surf League.

He has covered government and corporate events including assignments at Rashtrapati Bhawan, international conferences, CSR summits, and major sporting championships across India and internationally.

As a national karting champion and endurance racer, Rachit uniquely understands speed, emotion, and timing which allows him to capture action content authentically.

He is also the Founder & Director of Hotshoe Pvt Ltd providing photography, films, live streaming, and media production. Certified drone pilot in Europe (EASA) and Dubai (DCAA). His work is driven by emotion, detail, and complete client satisfaction.

FAQ

Common Questions

Where are you based, and do you travel for assignments?

I am based in New Delhi, India, but I work extensively across India and internationally. I have covered projects in 13+ countries including UAE, Europe, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East. For international assignments, travel and accommodation arrangements can be discussed during the booking process.

Can you work on international projects?

Yes, I regularly work on international projects. I am a certified drone pilot in Europe (EASA) and Dubai (DCAA), which allows me to provide aerial services in these regions legally. I have worked with global brands like Red Bull, World Surf League, and covered events at international venues.

Do you offer drone photography services?

Yes, I offer professional drone photography and cinematography services. I hold certifications from EASA (European Union Aviation Safety Agency) and DCAA (Dubai Civil Aviation Authority). Drone services include aerial photography, videography, real estate surveys, and event coverage from unique perspectives.

How can brands collaborate with you?

Brands can collaborate through various models: project-based assignments, retainer partnerships, event coverage contracts, or content creation packages. I work with the brand's marketing team to understand objectives and deliver visual content that aligns with their brand identity and campaign goals. Contact me to discuss your specific requirements.

What is your booking process?

The booking process is simple: (1) Contact me via the form, WhatsApp, or phone with your project details. (2) We discuss requirements, timeline, and budget. (3) I provide a detailed quote and contract. (4) Upon confirmation and advance payment, the date is blocked. (5) Shoot execution and post-production follow the agreed timeline. (6) Final delivery in your preferred format.
